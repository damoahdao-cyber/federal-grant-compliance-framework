## Resource checks

The starter workbook was recalculated using the authoring calculation engine. Baseline totals were compared with separately specified expected values. Input-change checks covered a zero budget, missing forecast input, the next portfolio row, changed SEFA subrecipient amounts, and zero, missing, or negative allocation units. Temporary inputs were restored before export.

A formula-error scan found no matching formula errors in the restored workbook. Every worksheet was rendered and inspected for readable labels, values, and layout. Exported workbook records and document links were checked before saving.

These are limited development checks. Native desktop Excel or Google Sheets behavior, operational use, full regulatory coverage, and empirical effectiveness have not been independently validated.
