# Independent practitioner review brief

Status: review materials prepared. No independent review response or institutional adoption is documented.

## Review scope

Review the initial Domain 1 resources, [workbook instructions](../../framework/01-financial-management/workbook-guide.md), and [synthetic examples](../../examples/synthetic-award-walkthrough.md). Record the exact commit or release reviewed. The other six domains are scope pages and should not be evaluated as completed operating resources.

Suggested reviewers have relevant experience in nonprofit finance, federal award administration, research administration, internal controls, or single audit preparation. Record their actual role and relevant experience with their permission. Disclose relationships with the project lead, participation in development, compensation, and other conflicts. Independence must be described accurately.

## Review tasks

1. Trace one award budget and one portfolio exception using the synthetic examples.
2. Reproduce the shared direct cost allocation and evaluate the proposed documentation requirements.
3. Assess the SEFA working schedule and its stated limits against the applicable requirements.
4. Check clarity, completeness within stated scope, regulatory references, practical usability, and material risks of misinterpretation.
5. Identify corrections by file, worksheet, and cell or section. Separate technical defects from preferences and out-of-scope feature requests.

Use the [feedback form](feedback-form.md). The form is a structured review aid, not a validated questionnaire or scoring instrument. There is no pass/fail certification threshold.

## Retaining actual evidence

Keep the original response, review date, exact reviewed version, and correction record. Confirm permission before publishing a reviewer's name, affiliation, quotation, or endorsement. Redact sensitive award, financial, personnel, and client information. Public GitHub issues must use synthetic or authorized material.

Only claim independent review after real feedback exists. Do not convert informal comments into institutional endorsement. If feedback is private, describe its scope accurately and retain the original record separately rather than inventing a public artifact.

## Follow-up

Log each material issue, resolution, revised version, and any remaining disagreement. Ask the reviewer to confirm significant corrections when feasible. Update [STATUS.md](../../STATUS.md) only with supported facts. Recruitment and person-directed outreach require separately identified recipients and authorization.
