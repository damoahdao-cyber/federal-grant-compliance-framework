# Contributing to the Federal Grant Compliance Framework

Contributions are welcome from nonprofit finance, grants management, sponsored programs, audit, and compliance professionals.

## Before contributing

1. Read the [README](README.md) for the current development status and planned domains.
2. Review existing files and [open issues](https://github.com/damoahdao-cyber/federal-grant-compliance-framework/issues) to avoid duplicate work.
3. Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
4. Review the [CC BY 4.0 license](LICENSE).

## Useful contributions

- Corrections to explanations, citations, and links.
- Domain guides, policy templates, and checklists.
- Editable financial trackers and reporting tools.
- Worked examples using synthetic data.
- Technical review and usability feedback.
- Translations preserving the meaning of the source material.

The framework remains in development. Consult the README before assuming a domain or template is complete.

## Submission process

For a small correction, fork the repository, make the change, and submit a pull request.

For a new resource or substantial change, open an issue describing the proposal before preparing the submission.

A pull request should explain:

- The problem addressed and the resulting change.
- The relevant framework domain.
- Sources supporting regulatory statements.
- Checks performed for templates, calculations, or examples.
- Any related issue.

The project lead reviews submissions before merging. Additional technical review is sought when appropriate.

## Content standards

- Use clear instructions and define technical terms.
- Cite applicable requirements and identify the date or version reviewed.
- Distinguish federal requirements, award-specific conditions, and suggested practices.
- Check calculations and examples before submission.
- Keep templates editable and document required software.
- Use accessible headings, descriptive image text, and readable formatting.
- Identify draft materials and limits of any evaluation.

Do not submit confidential organizational information, personal records, unauthorized copyrighted material, promotional content, or misleading compliance claims.

## Licensing

By submitting a contribution, you confirm you have the right to share it and agree to license your original contribution under CC BY 4.0.

Identify third-party material and retain its required attribution and license notices. Earlier material distributed under CC0 retains its existing permissions.

## Feedback and questions

Open an issue describing the affected file, the concern, and the proposed correction. For calculation or template problems, include a synthetic example and the expected result.
