# Domain 2 maintainer and practitioner review checklist

Review against the exact branch, commit, and award/regulatory version. Completion of this checklist is not an independent certification.

## Maintainer checks
- [ ] Six procedures and their scope are identifiable and accurately indexed.
- [ ] Every source URL resolves to the intended section; record date and source edition.
- [ ] Verify sections and paragraph-level requirements against applicable eCFR text and agency implementation.
- [ ] Verify thresholds and their effective dates; account for organization-level lower thresholds or authorized variants.
- [ ] Check privacy instructions and ensure there are no real sensitive records in examples.
- [ ] Verify arithmetic in synthetic examples and avoid implied allowability conclusions.
- [ ] Verify cross-folder links and no duplicated, conflicting instructions.
- [ ] Identify any resource requiring extra coverage before organizational implementation.

## Practitioner review prompts
1. Which procedure conflicts with agency, recipient type, state, or organizational requirements?
2. What missing decision points or records create the greatest implementation risks?
3. Are the controls and reviewer roles practicable in a small nonprofit or research institution?
4. Are the examples accurate and understandable without suggesting automatic approval?
5. What additional policies must be added before recommending organizational use?
