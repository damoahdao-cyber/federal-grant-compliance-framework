# Travel and expense review procedure

## Purpose
A suggested review procedure for travel costs on federal awards. Apply the organization's established written travel policy consistently to federal and nonfederal activities, while checking award conditions and relevant statutory travel requirements.

## Before travel
1. Document the traveler, award, destination, dates, purpose, and necessity for the scope of work.
2. Review approved budget, written policy, applicable agency restrictions and any required preauthorization.
3. Select the travel reimbursement method for the trip, such as actual cost, per diem, mileage, or a permitted combination. Apply the selected method consistently for the trip rather than selectively by day.
4. Review airfare class, itinerary, lodging, meals, incidentals, accessibility needs, and any special rules relevant to the trip. Do not treat an internal approval as a waiver of federal restrictions.

## After travel
1. Match receipts or permitted supporting records to the expense claim, dates, activity, approved purposes, and organization's rates or ceilings.
2. Assess necessity, reasonableness, and conformity to established written policy, especially when lodging or subsistence is charged directly.
3. Document any unusual airfare or accommodation justification at the time of approval and review exceptions against governing rules.
4. Reconcile advances, reimbursements, refunds, and duplicate claims. Record final coding, preparer/reviewer, and posting date.

## Limitations
Conference attendance or a travel budget alone does not establish eligibility for reimbursement. Specialized restrictions, international travel rules, and agency terms may apply. The organizational policy governs ordinary rates where consistent with applicable federal requirements; absent a written policy, 2 CFR 200.475(d) identifies a federal rate basis.
