# Federal award records retention and access procedure

## Purpose
A starter procedure for establishing retention triggers and secure storage for award records. A single fixed date for all documents is inappropriate because the applicable trigger and extensions vary by record type, award, and circumstances.

## Procedure
1. **Classify records.** Identify financial records, supporting documents, procurement files, personnel support, performance records, property records, indirect-rate proposals, and related decisions.
2. **Determine the applicable clock.** For ordinary award records under 2 CFR 200.334, the general period is three years from submission of the final financial report. For awards renewed quarterly or annually, the relevant reporting submissions establish the period as described in the regulation.
3. **Identify exceptions.** Record active litigation, claims, or audit findings that began before the original period expired, written retention extensions, property and equipment final disposition, post-period program income, and special indirect cost calculations.
4. **Compare longer obligations.** Apply the specific exceptions permitted by 2 CFR 200.334. Do not assume federal agencies and pass-through entities have unrestricted authority to impose additional retention periods. Evaluate any independent state-law, contract, organizational, privacy, security, or litigation-hold requirements with the relevant records officer. Escalate conflicts rather than silently deleting data.
5. **Protect and retrieve.** Restrict access based on role, preserve integrity and metadata, document where records are stored, and provide authorized access under applicable requirements. Avoid sharing protected personal or grant information in public repositories.
6. **Dispose with authorization.** Confirm the correct trigger date, all extensions or holds, approvals, and retained disposition evidence before any destruction.

## Suggested closure record
Record the governing provision or award term, source documents, final trigger calculation, hold clearance, reviewer, and authorized disposition date. If uncertain, preserve and escalate to the organization's records or legal owner instead of making a unilateral destruction decision.

## Primary references
[2 CFR 200.334](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.334) (record retention exceptions) and [200.337](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.337) (access to records). Check federal agency implementation and additional applicable legal preservation requirements.
