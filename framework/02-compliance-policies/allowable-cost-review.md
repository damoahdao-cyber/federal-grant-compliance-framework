# Allowable cost review procedure

## Purpose and scope
A reusable decision record for proposed direct charges to a U.S. federal award. It does not determine allowability automatically. Use the award's applicable regulatory version, statutes, agency requirements, terms, approved budget, and organizational policy.

## Procedure
1. **Identify the transaction.** Record award identifier, expense purpose, vendor or employee, date, accounting period, amount, ledger reference, and requested funding source. Keep source documents in the organization's approved system.
2. **Confirm benefit and timing.** Describe how the expense benefits the award or other cost objective, and whether it falls in the applicable budget period. Assess shared benefit using a documented allocation rather than charging an arbitrary project.
3. **Evaluate the cost principles.** Document necessity, reasonableness, allocability, award or regulatory restrictions, policy consistency, consistent direct/indirect treatment, duplicate funding or cost sharing, and adequate supporting records.
4. **Check category-specific rules.** For personnel, travel, equipment, procurement, entertainment, or other special cost categories, consult relevant provisions and any prior-approval requirements.
5. **Resolve credits and exceptions.** Review refunds, rebates, discounts, or other applicable credits. Escalate unclear or restricted costs for an authorized written determination before posting or reimbursement.
6. **Record disposition.** Approve, hold for clarification, redirect to a different appropriate cost objective, or reject the proposed charge. Retain preparer/reviewer identity and dated evidence of the actual disposition.

## Control points
- A budget balance, purchase approval, or accounting code alone does not establish federal allowability.
- Do not shift an expense to another federal award solely to address an overspend or restriction.
- Do not publish real invoices, employee records, or award identifiers in public GitHub issues. Use synthetic examples.
- Coordinate shared charges with the [Domain 1 allocation methodology](../01-financial-management/cost-allocation-methodology.md).

## Governing references
[2 CFR 200.403](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.403) (allowability), [200.404](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.404) (reasonableness), [200.405](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.405) (allocability), [200.406](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.406) (applicable credits), [200.407](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.407) (prior written approval), and [200.410](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.410) (unallowable costs). The decision record is a suggested organizational control, not a federally prescribed form.
