# Domain 2: Compliance Policies and Procedures

Status: planned. This page describes development scope. No operational domain resources are published in this folder.

## Intended scope

Allowable cost policies, procurement procedures, personnel cost documentation, travel, conflicts of interest, and records retention.

Coordinate this domain with the cost allocation and cash procedures in Domain 1. Identify applicable organizational and award-specific requirements before preparing policies.

## Work required before publication

Prepare original materials, cite applicable requirements, check examples, and seek practitioner feedback. Document the checks performed and distinguish them from independent review or pilot evaluation.
