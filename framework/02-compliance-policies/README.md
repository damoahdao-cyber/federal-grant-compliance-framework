# Domain 2: Compliance Policies and Procedures

An early development set for nonprofit and research organizations administering U.S. federal awards. These are adaptable procedural starters, not organization-approved policies or independent compliance determinations.

## Use and limitations

1. Determine the federal award's applicable legal and agency rules, effective version, entity type, and award-specific conditions.
2. Review the corresponding draft procedure and document local responsibilities, approvals, records, and exceptions.
3. Verify each citation and adapt the template to current internal policy.
4. Pilot internally using only authorized records and qualified oversight, after appropriate approval.
5. Log defects and revisions with the exact version reviewed.

No practitioner review, organization-specific legal adaptation, operational field test, or demonstrated compliance outcome is claimed for these drafts. A developed resource is not equivalent to a validated institutional policy. The synthetic examples are illustrative and do not use recipient information.

This domain coordinates with [Domain 1 financial management](../01-financial-management/README.md), particularly its cost allocation, financial reporting and award monitoring guidance.
