# Federal award procurement procedure starter

## Purpose and prerequisites
Use as a starting procedure for purchasing goods or services charged to federal awards. Adopt only after confirming the recipient type, award conditions, applicable procurement rules, state/local/tribal requirements, and the organization's stricter written policy. Do not confuse a federal recipient's procurement standards with direct federal agency contracting rules.

## Procedure
1. **Define need and funding.** Record the specification, estimated full transaction value, timeline, award, budget authorization, and whether acquisition is necessary or duplicative.
2. **Choose the method before solicitation.** Check the applicable micro-purchase and simplified acquisition thresholds and organizational limits. Document the method chosen: micro-purchase, simplified acquisition, formal sealed bid or proposal, or an expressly permitted noncompetitive method.
3. **Apply competition requirements.** For micro-purchases, document price reasonableness even when quotations are not solicited, and distribute purchases equitably where practicable. For simplified acquisitions, obtain price or rate quotations from an adequate number of qualified sources. For formal procurement, follow the applicable competitive process and written evaluation standards.
4. **Handle noncompetitive requests cautiously.** Establish and retain which exception in 2 CFR 200.320(c), if any, applies and its supporting evidence. Convenience or late planning alone is not a substitute for an allowable exception.
5. **Check conflicts and contractor eligibility.** Obtain disclosures from decision-makers, evaluate responsibility and relevant exclusion or debarment requirements, and record evaluations without exposing sensitive information.
6. **Evaluate and select.** Record solicitation, bids or quotations, procurement-method justification, price or cost analysis when applicable, selection or rejection rationale, contract type, required terms, and approval.
7. **Administer the contract.** Verify deliverables and invoices against the agreement, monitor performance, retain change orders and disputes, and close the procurement file after final review.

## Threshold review
As of October 13, 2026, general FAR thresholds are $15,000 (micro-purchase) and $350,000 (simplified acquisition), following changes effective October 1, 2025. These are **not automatic authority** to use those limits for every award or recipient. 2 CFR 200.320 permits lower entity thresholds and certain documented higher micro-purchase thresholds. Recheck the current FAR, the award's applicable rules, agency guidance, recipient type, and internal policy before determining the method. Do not split a procurement to avoid applicable competition requirements.
