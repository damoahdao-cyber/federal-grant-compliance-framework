# Domain 2 synthetic walkthroughs

All figures and parties below are fictional. These are decision-documentation illustrations, not approval decisions or compliance test evidence.

## Example A: cost benefitting two objectives

A fictional $1,500 supply charge benefits Award A and an institutional activity. Usage logs show 60% and 40% relative benefit. A candidate proportional allocation is $900 to Award A and $600 to the institutional activity.

Review steps: verify the specific purchase is necessary, reasonable, permitted and adequately documented; verify whether the usage log is a supportable basis; check any credits or excluded cost components; record approval and ledger references. A $900 charge is not automatically allowable. Refer to [allowable cost review](allowable-cost-review.md) and [Domain 1 cost allocation](../01-financial-management/cost-allocation-methodology.md).

## Example B: proposed sole-source purchase

A fictional procurement officer proposes selecting Vendor Z without competition for an award-related service. The officer must review the estimated full purchase value, written organizational thresholds, the possible 2 CFR 200.320(c) exceptions, vendor responsibility, conflicts, price support, and required records before making any commitment.

A preferred vendor or late planning is not itself an adequate noncompetitive justification. The scenario has no outcome because sufficient procurement facts are deliberately unspecified. Refer to [procurement procedures](procurement-procedures.md).

## Example C: labor redistribution

For a fictional $8,000 salary period, supported activity shares of 60%, 25%, and 15% would yield $4,800, $2,000, and $1,200. The totals reconcile to $8,000. Award eligibility, work performed records, salary restrictions, and reconciliation must be evaluated separately. Refer to [personnel cost documentation](personnel-cost-documentation.md).

## Example D: record retention trigger

A fictional final financial report is submitted on January 31, 2026. A general three-year clock would reach January 31, 2029, but no destruction date is established until the specific award, exceptions, holds, and other controlling requirements have been reviewed. Refer to [records retention](records-retention.md).

Arithmetic check: 1,500 × 0.60 = 900; 1,500 × 0.40 = 600; 8,000 × 0.60 = 4,800; 8,000 × 0.25 = 2,000; 8,000 × 0.15 = 1,200; allocations each reconcile to their total.
