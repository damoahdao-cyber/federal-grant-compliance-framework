# Domain 3 synthetic worked examples

All organizations, awards, people, program criteria and activity records below are fabricated. Examples demonstrate documentation workflows, not federal program criteria, authorization to collect personal data, award eligibility, or actual achievements.

## Scenario A: an invented training program eligibility rule
For illustration only, a fictional grant defines two explicit prerequisites **in its hypothetical program rules**: a valid application during the program enrollment window, and an approved training registration record before participation. These are not universal federal eligibility conditions.

Eight pseudonymous cases are submitted. Review records show:
- **5 eligible**, with both fictional criteria documented;
- **2 pending**, because one necessary enrollment item in each case remains unverified;
- **1 ineligible**, based on a documented failure to meet an explicit fictional program rule, with notification and reconsideration route handled according to the hypothetical program procedure.

Total = 5 + 2 + 1 = **8**. Eligible share among all submissions = 5 / 8 = **62.5%**, a synthetic descriptive metric, not an acceptance target or fairness result. The two pending cases must not be recoded as ineligible or as verified eligible.

Record: source rule version, internal pseudonymous ID, evidence pointer, decision date, authorized reviewer and any appeal or correction. Do not publicly store identifiable participant records.

## Scenario B: milestone progress
A fictional award requires **12 documented training sessions** within a specified reporting interval. By the reporting date, **7** sessions have documented completion and **5** remain.

Progress = 7 / 12 × 100 = **58.33%** (rounded to two decimal places). Progress is a descriptive indicator only. It does not establish allowability, quality, compliance with a contract deadline, or completion of the award. Confirm that no canceled or merely scheduled sessions were counted.

## Scenario C: proposed program expansion
An award supports a named research activity. The team proposes adding an unrelated new activity without raising the total budget. The project manager identifies a likely change in the approved scope or objective, checks [2 CFR 200.308(f)(1)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.308), and holds the change pending external **written** approval if required. Budget neutrality does not remove this approval trigger.

## Scenario D: organizational eligibility versus covered lower-tier transaction
A fictional institution applies for a federal award. It checks the actual NOFO eligibility, entity registration rules, submission deadlines and any exemptions. Later it proposes a subaward to a different organization. The subaward is not an individual participant determination: pass-through requirements under 2 CFR 200.332 and applicable 180.300 lower-tier exclusion methods need review, with further guidance in Domain 5.

## Arithmetic validation
The expected synthetic values are: eight reviewed cases in all, five eligible, two pending, one ineligible, a 62.5% eligible-case share, twelve planned sessions, seven evidenced sessions, five remaining, and a 58.33% session completion share. No real participant or project data are represented.
