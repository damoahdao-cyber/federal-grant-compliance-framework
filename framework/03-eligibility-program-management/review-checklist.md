# Domain 3 maintainer and independent practitioner review

Record the actual reviewer, dated repository commit, precise award/regulatory version, supporting evidence and conflicts. This checklist is not a validated assessment instrument. Blank rows are not reviews.

## Maintainer integrity checks
- [ ] Each procedure contains scope, decision owner, source, limits, attribution and a review status.
- [ ] Program and applicant eligibility are distinct from individual participant eligibility and lower-tier transaction screening.
- [ ] No universal requirement to collect identity, citizenship, demographic or medical data was invented.
- [ ] 2 CFR 200.205–200.206 agency responsibilities are not reassigned to applicants/recipients.
- [ ] Covered transaction alternatives under 2 CFR 180.300 are accurately stated.
- [ ] 2 CFR 200.308 scope change approvals are distinguished from budget-only controls.
- [ ] Synthetic arithmetic, reproducible statuses, hyperlinks and file names are checked.
- [ ] Any real participant or institutional data are absent from public resources.
- [ ] Exact effective regulatory text and agency-specific eligibility rules are verified before operational adoption.

## Practitioner review prompts
1. Which applicant or participant decision points need additional program-specific authority, evidence, safeguards or appeal handling?
2. Does the intake approach appropriately represent federal award terms and specific conditions?
3. Would a nonprofit or research administrator be able to track an eligibility decision and a completed service back to authorized records?
4. What privacy, nondiscrimination, accessibility or recordkeeping risks remain?
5. Do program performance monitoring and prior-approval steps create a misleading impression of completed legal compliance?
6. Which Domain 3 topics overlap Domain 2 procedures, Domain 4 reporting, or Domain 5 subrecipient oversight and should be revised?
