# Federal award intake and implementation readiness

## Purpose
A suggested internal intake record for an organization receiving federal financial assistance. This is **not** the federal agency's merit review or 200.206 applicant risk assessment, and does not authorize accepting prohibited award terms.

## Intake procedure
1. **Authenticate the award.** Record award notice and amendment versions, awarding agency, recipient legal name, UEI, FAIN, Assistance Listing, and governing contacts from authoritative award documents.
2. **Classify funding.** Identify award type, approved program description, performance and budget periods, funding obligated, approved budget, applicable cost sharing, indirect cost treatment, and required reports.
3. **Extract obligations.** Record performance objectives, measurable outputs, participant rules where applicable, reporting cadence and deliverables, program restrictions, prior-approval triggers, specific conditions under section 200.208, and applicable agency terms.
4. **Assign accountable owners.** Establish program director, grants office, finance, compliance, data steward and approver. Document responsibilities and backup contacts without inserting private contact details in GitHub.
5. **Confirm readiness.** Review necessary staffing and systems, data-handling plan, permissions, approvals, monitoring schedule, and dependencies with subrecipients or vendors. Escalate unresolved restrictions before activities begin.
6. **Approve the operating baseline.** Obtain actual organizational sign-off, preserve authoritative versions, and send required questions to the awarding agency or pass-through entity.

## Source boundaries
Under [2 CFR 200.211](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.211), federal awards contain specified identification, financial and award-condition information. The intake register is an optional internal aid to extract and assign that information. [2 CFR 200.208](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.208) governs specific award conditions; [2 CFR 200.301](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.301) concerns performance measurement. For initial applicant criteria see [2 CFR 200.204](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.204). Refer to [Domain 1](../01-financial-management/README.md) for financial supporting schedules.
