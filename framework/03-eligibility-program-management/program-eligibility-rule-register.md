# Program-specific eligibility rule register

## Purpose
Capture the precise rules for eligibility **before** anyone evaluates an individual or organization's participation in a funded activity. No universal eligibility standard is created here.

## Separate the three decisions
1. **Applicant or recipient eligibility:** Does the organization or application meet the funding opportunity and award conditions? Check the notice of funding opportunity (NOFO), authorizing program, and award. The federal agency performs its required merit and risk reviews under 2 CFR 200.205 and 200.206; this worksheet is for the applicant's internal readiness only.
2. **Participant or beneficiary eligibility:** Does a person, family, project, or service recipient meet the *specific program's* conditions? Some research grants have no individual beneficiary eligibility determination. Do not invent one.
3. **Subrecipient or contractor screening:** Apply appropriate lower-tier award or procurement procedures, not individual beneficiary checks. See [Domain 2 procurement](../02-compliance-policies/procurement-procedures.md) and future Domain 5. A covered lower-tier transaction uses applicable 2 CFR 180.300 methods.

## Establish the rules
1. Identify the Assistance Listing, federal agency, NOFO identifier and version, authorizing program, award ID, amendments, period of performance, and implementing requirements.
2. Extract each eligibility condition exactly into a restricted working register. Cite its location and effective date. Distinguish mandatory requirements, preferences, priority factors, and reporting classifications.
3. Identify whose eligibility is tested, at which point, what evidence is authorized, and what exceptions or appeal rights apply *under the program*.
4. Establish who verifies and who approves, with separation of duties where practicable. Identify accessible alternatives where permitted, but do not waive eligibility rules without authority.
5. Record legal, privacy, records-retention, nondiscrimination, data quality, and program-specific limitations. Collect only necessary information.
6. Approve the rule register before using it. Review when a relevant rule, award term, or program policy changes, and record effective dates.

## Pre-use questions
- Does this award **actually** have participant eligibility criteria, or only program activity conditions?
- Does a citizenship, residency, age, income, organizational status, or other characteristic appear in the controlling requirements? Do not infer such restrictions from the federal funding source alone.
- Is an agency or program-specific determination required before services or payments begin?
- Is the program applying the correct regulatory edition for its award?
- Will a decision or the eligibility record involve sensitive data requiring restricted handling?

## References and boundaries
[2 CFR 200.204](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.204) (NOFO), [Appendix I to 2 CFR part 200](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/part-200/appendix-Appendix%20I%20to%20Part%20200) (notice eligibility content), [2 CFR 200.211](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.211) (award content), [2 CFR 25.200](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-25.200) (applicant registration, subject to exemptions), [2 CFR 200.303](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.303) (controls and safeguards). These rules alone do not impose a universal individual eligibility test.
