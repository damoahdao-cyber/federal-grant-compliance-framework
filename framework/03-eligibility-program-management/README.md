# Domain 3: Eligibility Verification and Program Management

Initial draft starter resources for federal award intake, **program-specific** eligibility determination, secure service-delivery records, approved workplans and program-change control. This is development material and does not establish a universal eligibility test, independently reviewed operating procedure, or evidence of organizational adoption.

## How to use
1. Open the actual federal award, incorporated conditions, agency guidance and effective program rules.
2. Decide whether applicant, individual beneficiary, project, or lower-tier transaction eligibility is even relevant. These categories have **different** legal responsibilities.
3. Complete the source-based rules register **before** applying a program decision procedure.
4. Establish secure record locations, owners, approvals and data access. Use actual records only inside authorized institutional systems.
5. Tie approved activities to source evidence and an operating workplan.
6. Log scope changes and applicable approvals before changing authorized activities.
7. Record feedback, revisions and review dates.

## Exclusions and limitations
- Uniform Guidance does not establish a single nationwide set of eligibility criteria for every grant beneficiary. Program statutes, award terms and implementing rules control.
- This package contains Markdown guidance and editable text tables, **not** a secure intake application, identity-check service, legal opinion, regulatory certification, or production database.
- General federal applicant risk review under 2 CFR 200.206 is primarily an **agency** function, not a beneficiary screening protocol.
- Subrecipient exclusion and monitoring belong chiefly in future Domain 5. Contract procurement belongs in [Domain 2](../02-compliance-policies/README.md).
- Domain 4 will address detailed performance report production. Domain 1 remains the financial supporting schedule.
- No individual names, sensitive records, financial information, or protected personal details should be uploaded to GitHub. The worked examples are entirely synthetic.
- No practitioner review, field trial, applicant determination, or demonstrated outcomes are claimed.
