# Program workplan, milestone and monitoring guide

## Purpose
Translate the approved award's project goals, activities, timeline and reporting obligations into an accountable operating plan. It does not alter the federal award or define new federally required performance metrics.

## Build an approved baseline
1. Extract each authorized objective and deliverable from the award, the incorporated work plan, and valid amendments. Name its source, accepted method of measurement, units, target, time period and authorized owner.
2. Define a scheduled review for activities, resources, dependencies, expenditures, and the state of required approvals. Use frequency appropriate to risks and actual award requirements.
3. Record the difference between **planned, in progress, completed, held, and cancelled**. Completion requires retained evidence, not merely a status entered by staff.
4. Compare actual accomplishments with approved objectives and budgets in the same reporting period, flagging delays, overages, unallowable requests, unexpected scope changes, and unsatisfied conditions.
5. Escalate material deviations, obtain any prior written approvals required by the award and 2 CFR 200.308, and document a corrective action owner, due date and completion evidence.
6. Reconcile workplan figures to source activity logs and eventual program performance reports. Record definitions and versions so reviewers reproduce reported totals.

## Monitoring reminders
- Under 2 CFR 200.329(a), recipients and subrecipients monitor award activities for compliance and performance expectations; this includes every relevant program, function or activity.
- Section 200.301 addresses agency performance measurement and required award communication. Use indicators actually included in the award or valid organizational internal management indicators clearly labeled as internal.
- Under 200.329, performance report content and timing are governed by the award and applicable rules. Do not treat a workplan review as an agency-submitted report.
- Never use a percentage alone to establish achievement of a qualitative objective; define and retain supporting evidence.
- Identify cost-related differences with the same period and source as the workplan, using [Domain 1](../01-financial-management/README.md).
