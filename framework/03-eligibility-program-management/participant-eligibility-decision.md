# Participant or beneficiary eligibility review

## Use only where program-specific rules apply
This suggested workflow is intended for programs that condition service or benefit delivery on recipient eligibility. A research award with no participant eligibility rule should record **not applicable**, not manufacture identity or demographic screening. Refer first to the [rule register](program-eligibility-rule-register.md).

## Suggested controlled workflow
1. Assign a restricted internal case reference. Explain the relevant program criteria, authorized evidence and any permitted accommodations or alternate documentation.
2. Match each applicable rule to its governing authority, effective date and necessary verification. Check that evidence is current for the decision date when required.
3. Separate *unverified or missing evidence* from *confirmed ineligibility*. Record pending cases for appropriate follow-up rather than making unsupported negative determinations.
4. Record a determination only by the authorized decision-maker. Possible statuses are **eligible, pending additional review, ineligible, not applicable**. Reasons must cite a specific rule and observed evidence; do not rely on assumptions or stereotypes.
5. Communicate the decision, permitted appeal or reconsideration route, and timing according to actual program requirements. Preserve the original decision and an audit trail of any later revision.
6. Before recording service or payment as eligible, verify any conditions that must be satisfied at that stage, and retain reviewer sign-off and approved supporting records.

## Privacy and fairness controls
- Do not put real participant files, names, identity documents, addresses, dates of birth, income statements, medical records, or personal identifiers into GitHub.
- Store source records securely with authorized access and institutional retention policies. A public spreadsheet with anonymized-looking IDs can still expose identifiable people through combination with other data.
- Avoid unnecessary collection of protected characteristics. Apply nondiscrimination, access and accommodation requirements relevant to the funded program.
- These templates do not authorize screening based on citizenship, national origin, disability, age or income unless the applicable program explicitly supplies such criteria and lawful procedures.
- Treat an appeal and an exception as different steps. Do not label a person eligible merely because an exception was requested.

## References
