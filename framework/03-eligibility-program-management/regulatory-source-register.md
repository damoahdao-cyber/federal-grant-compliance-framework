Do not treat a link to the *current* eCFR page as proof that the current wording governs every existing award. Record the applicable award date, program authority, agency rules, terms, and written amendments. Proposed rules do not supersede binding conditions.

### Important boundary: 2 CFR 180.300
For a covered next-lower-tier transaction, 180.300 allows three verification methods: checking SAM.gov exclusions, collecting a certification, **or** incorporating an appropriate clause or condition. A SAM search is therefore not the only method permitted by the general rule; other agency or award instructions may impose additional checks. Do not apply 180.300 as a general individual-beneficiary eligibility test.
