# Shared direct cost allocation methodology

Use the workbook's Cost allocation worksheet for a shared direct cost benefiting several activities. It does not calculate an indirect cost rate or certify personnel effort.

## Method

1. Identify the cost, accounting period, invoice or ledger reference, and benefiting activities.
2. Check necessity, reasonableness, award restrictions, and consistent direct or indirect treatment.
3. Select a supportable measure of relative benefit, such as documented equipment-use hours.
4. Include all activities receiving the benefit, including nonfederal activities.
5. Divide each activity's units by total units. Multiply the share by the allowable cost pool.
6. Reconcile allocated amounts with the original cost. Document any rounding adjustment and obtain review before posting.

The synthetic illustration allocates a $12,000 equipment-related cost using 60, 30, and 10 usage hours. It allocates $7,200, $3,600, and $1,200. The benefit measure is an illustration, not an accepted universal allocation base.

Do not move costs between awards to solve budget shortages or bypass restrictions. An allocation calculation does not establish allowability. Maintain the applicable indirect rate and base separately.
