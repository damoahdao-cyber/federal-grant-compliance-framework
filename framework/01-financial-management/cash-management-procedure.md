## Advance request

Estimate immediate allowable disbursements. Account for available award cash and other funds required to be used before requesting more. Limit the request to the documented near-term cash need. A budget balance does not represent an immediate cash need.

## Reimbursement request

Prepare the applicable incurred-cost or paid-cost support required by the payment system and award terms. Reconcile eligible costs, prior requests, receipts, and adjustments. Do not request the same costs twice. This procedure does not set one accounting basis for every reimbursement arrangement.

## Approval and reconciliation

The preparer retains the supporting schedule. A separate approver checks eligibility, timing, amounts, and prior payments before submission. Record the request confirmation and receipt. Reconcile the award cash position and investigate excess balances or delayed payments. Review applicable interest rules and exceptions using the full source provision.
