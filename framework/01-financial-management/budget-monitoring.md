# Budget monitoring guide

Use the workbook's Budget review worksheet for one federal-share approved budget period. Maintain fiscal-year views separately when needed. Do not compare a multiyear award ceiling with only a single year's expenditure and label the difference a period variance.

## Preparation

Confirm the current approved budget, funding share, cutoff, and accounting basis. Reconcile ledger expenditures and adjustments. Remove settled commitments from the open-obligation list. Estimate remaining uncommitted costs and retain assumptions for personnel, fringe, travel, supplies, subawards, and indirect costs.

The remaining forecast excludes open obligations. The forecast total adds them once. Compare projected expenditure with the authorized funding for the same scope.

A forecast shortfall prompts a documented funding or scope review. A positive balance does not authorize a change in scope or a transfer between categories. Record approval requirements and obtain applicable written approvals before making restricted revisions.

Use explanations supported by staffing plans, procurement commitments, or program changes. Separate timing differences from projected total cost changes. Record preparer and reviewer sign-off with the retained forecast file.
