# Recurring award financial review checklist

Suggested cadence: monthly, adjusted for award risk and reporting requirements. This cadence is a management choice rather than a universal federal monthly requirement.

Award ID: ______  Review period: ______  Cutoff: ______  Preparer: ______  Reviewer: ______

Use Complete, Open, or Not applicable for each item. Explain each Not applicable response. Attach evidence and assign outstanding actions.

Record exceptions in the action table in [budget-monitoring.md](budget-monitoring.md). Financial review does not replace the later procurement, subrecipient monitoring, or personnel documentation domains.

Sources: [2 CFR 200.303](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.303), documented controls, monitoring, and corrective action. [2 CFR 200.403](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.403), cost documentation. The checklist is a suggested review tool.
