# Domain 1: Financial Management and Reporting

An initial financial management starter pack for nonprofit and research institution finance and grants teams. These resources were developed on September 30, 2026. They are available for adaptation and practitioner review.

## Resources

| Resource | Purpose |
| --- | --- |

| [Workbook instructions](workbook-guide.md) | Inputs, formulas, record limits, and synthetic control totals |
| [Award register](award-register.md) | Capture award identifiers, dates, approvals, and financial record sources |

| [Financial reporting checklist](financial-reporting-checklist.md) | Review reporting basis, award conditions, support, and submission evidence |

## Start here

1. Read the workbook instructions and select the relevant supporting schedule.
2. Save a working copy and replace the synthetic inputs with authorized data.
3. Complete the award register and preserve the source records.
4. Review applicable award terms and the sources cited in each guide.
5. Reconcile calculations and document preparer and reviewer sign-off.

## Scope and review status

The workbook is a collection of independent supporting schedules. It does not import an accounting ledger, apply award-specific allowability decisions, calculate an indirect cost rate, or produce a complete certified SEFA or SF-425 submission.

Calculation checks and worksheet layout review are documented in the [development record](../../docs/development-record.md). These checks do not establish empirical effectiveness, independent professional review, organizational adoption, or pilot results.

Further resources and revisions are planned. Domain 1 is an initial resource set, rather than a declaration of complete compliance coverage.
