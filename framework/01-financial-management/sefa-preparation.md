# SEFA preparation guide

The Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards is a fiscal-period schedule, rather than a list of authorized award ceilings. Use the workbook's SEFA preparation worksheet as a supporting workpaper for ordinary expenditure-based awards.

## Preparation and review

| Task | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- |
| Confirm the organizational scope, fiscal period, and accounting basis | Financial-statement scope and accounting policy |
| Identify federal awards, including pass-through awards | Award records and ledger mapping |
| Verify agencies, programs, Assistance Listing identifiers, and pass-through identifiers | Source agreements and program records |
| Reconcile ordinary expenditures to the ledger | Detail listing and adjustment explanations |
| Prepare individual program and applicable cluster totals | Grouping workpaper, including appropriate R&D presentation |
| Identify amounts provided to subrecipients by program | Supported disbursement and classification records |
| Address loans, noncash assistance, insurance, and other special cases separately | Applicable 200.502 workpapers |
| Prepare accounting-policy and applicable indirect-rate election notes | Draft notes and policy support |
| Reconcile totals and obtain review | Reconciliation, preparer, reviewer, and dates |

Own direct costs in the worksheet exclude indirect costs and amounts provided to subrecipients. Enter the latter separately to avoid double counting. Use the same fiscal-period cutoff and documented basis across the schedule. Do not derive the SEFA solely from grant cash receipts.

The synthetic workpaper totals $130,000, including $13,000 provided to subrecipients. It is not a final SEFA. Grouping, notes, adjustments, and special-assistance computations remain necessary where applicable.

Sources: [2 CFR 200.510(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.510), schedule structure and disclosures. [2 CFR 200.502](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.502), determination of federal awards expended and special forms of assistance.
