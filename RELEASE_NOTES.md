# Proposed release: v0.1.0-alpha

Publication status: prepared, not yet published as a GitHub release.

Release title: **FGCF v0.1.0-alpha: Initial financial management resources**

## Release body

This early development package provides initial financial management resources for nonprofit and research organizations administering U.S. federal awards.

Included:

- A financial management starter workbook with independent budget review, portfolio, SEFA preparation, and shared direct cost allocation schedules.
- Workbook instructions and seven supporting guides or templates.
- Seven domain directories, with initial resources in Domain 1 and scope pages in Domains 2 through 7.
- Three synthetic worked examples, explicit development status, and citation metadata.
- A revised design working paper with a proposed evaluation, roadmap, and practitioner review materials.

All seeded data are synthetic. Maintainer calculation and layout checks are documented in `docs/development-record.md`. Independent practitioner review, native spreadsheet application testing, operational testing, pilot evaluation, and adoption evidence remain pending. The working paper is not represented as a peer-reviewed journal publication.

This prerelease is for inspection, adaptation, and feedback. Review applicable regulations, agency guidance, award terms, and organization-specific requirements before use. The workbook does not generate a complete SEFA or implement an indirect cost rate agreement.
