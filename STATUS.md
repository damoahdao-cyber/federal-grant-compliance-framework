## What the workbook covers

The workbook contains independent schedules for budget review, portfolio balances, SEFA preparation, and allocation of shared direct costs. It uses synthetic inputs. It does not connect to a ledger, filter transaction dates, produce a complete SEFA, or implement an indirect cost rate agreement.

See the [workbook instructions](framework/01-financial-management/workbook-guide.md), [development record](docs/development-record.md), and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
